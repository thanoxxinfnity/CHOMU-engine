The engine

A complete game studio, in your pocket.

CHoMU Engine runs natively on Android. Create a project, lay out your scenes, write your gameplay and hit play — all on the same device.

Full editor on your phone

Scene tree, inspector, script editor, project manager and one-tap run. The whole workflow, no laptop required.

2D & 3D renderer

Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility renderers with real-time lights, shadows, fog, glow and HDRI skies.

Scripting built in

Write gameplay in GDScript with a built-in code editor, syntax highlighting and autocompletion.

Shaders & post-processing

Write custom shaders and materials, then tune tonemapping, glow and fog until your world looks right.

Physics & animation

2D and 3D physics, drivable vehicles, character controllers and an animation system for bringing scenes to life.

Made for Android

A native arm64 app for phones and tablets. Open project folders from your device storage and keep building anywhere.
